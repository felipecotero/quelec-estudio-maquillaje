Ten actual hair meshes and their original UVs and alpha textures are included.
The MakeHuman .mhclo barycentric references are applied to the same adult base
and normalization used by Quelec's source anatomy. Runtime fitting follows the
canonical head's displacement; the hair geometry is never substituted with
spheres, tubes, toroids, polyhedra, or a generated scalp cap.

PNG textures are resized to at most 1024 × 1024 while preserving alpha.
Runtime tinting changes strand color and preserves the authored strand texture
and alpha. JPG thumbnails are reduced copies of the source .thumb files.
mesh.json retains the source OBJ SHA256 and source identity. manifest.json
records mesh counts, bounds, texture sizes and alpha range.

These are hairstyle references fitted to an approximate digital head. They
do not claim to reconstruct an actor's photographed hairstyle exactly.
No MakeHuman application code is distributed.
